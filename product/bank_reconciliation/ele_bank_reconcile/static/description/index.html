Bank Reconciliation Match Quality

See at a glance which bank statement lines are safe to skip, and which ones actually need review.

The problem

A reconciled bank statement line is not the same as a correctly reconciled one. Some lines are obviously fine — a payment matched exactly to its invoice, a recognised bank fee, an internal transfer. Others are reconciled but only approximately, or matched against the wrong document entirely. Without a way to tell them apart, reviewers re-check every line, including the hundreds that were never in doubt.

What this app does

It adds a single computed field — Match Quality — to every bank statement line, with two values:

  • Perfect — every journal item on the transaction is backed by an invoice or bill, or the line is recognisable as a bank fee, internal transfer, or currency gain/loss (the kinds of transactions that legitimately have no invoice).
  • Partial — anything else. These are the lines worth a reviewer's time.

That badge shows up right inside the Bank Matching widget you already use to reconcile — its "Reconciled" indicator turns green for Perfect and orange for Partial, no extra screen to check.

Built for your chart of accounts, not ours

Every pattern this app matches on is a per-company setting, found under Accounting → Configuration → Settings → Bank Reconciliation:

Setting Purpose
Reconciliation Tolerance Accounts Account codes for small, accepted discrepancies (e.g. rounding, exchange differences).
Bank Charge Patterns Text patterns that identify bank fees in account names or transaction labels.
Internal Transfer Keywords Keywords that identify transfers between your own accounts.

Nothing is hardcoded to one language, bank, or country. Every setting falls back to sensible defaults, so the app works immediately after install and can be tuned to your business afterwards.

Why it's safe to install

  • Requires Odoo Enterprise's Accounting app (account_accountant) — the reconciliation widget it enhances only exists there.
  • Adds one field and recolours one badge you already see. It does not change how reconciliation works, only how its results are labelled.
  • Covered by automated tests, including an end-to-end check that tolerance detection honours your configured accounts.
